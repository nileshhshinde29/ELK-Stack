
**Q25. What are the key fields for creating observability dashboards?**

1. **Monitoring Identity:** `@timestamp`, `monitor.name`, `monitor.type`, `monitor.id`, `url.full`. Core dimensions for filtering, grouping, and time-series bucketing.
2. **Status & Availability:** `monitor.status`, `summary.up`, `summary.down`, `state.status`, `http.response.status_code`. Essential for calculating SLA/uptime percentages and error rates.
3. **Performance Metrics:** `monitor.duration.us`, `http.rtt.total.us`, `tcp.rtt.connect.us`, `tls.rtt.handshake.us`. Used for latency tracking, SLA breaches, and bottleneck identification.
4. **Network & Connection:** `resolve.ip`, `monitor.ip`, `observer.hostname`. Crucial for geo-mapping, load balancer routing issues, and isolating observer problems.
5. **Security & TLS:** `tls.established`, `tls.version`, `tls.server.x509.not_after`. Monitor SSL certificate expiry dates and enforce secure protocol versions.

**Q26. What are the practical dashboard visualizations?**

* **Global Uptime SLA:** Aggregates: `summary.up` / `state.checks`
* **Response Time Trends:** X: `@timestamp`, Y: `monitor.duration.us`
* **HTTP Status Distribution:** Terms: `http.response.status_code` (200, 404, 500)
* **SSL Cert Expiry Tracker:** Math: `tls.server.x509.not_after` - now (e.g., [www.elastic.co](http://www.elastic.co), 45 Days)
