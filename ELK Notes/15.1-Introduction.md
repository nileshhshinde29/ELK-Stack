## 5. Cluster Creation – Questions to Ask the Client

1. How much data are you ingesting into Elasticsearch **per day**?
2. What is the **retention** period for this data?
3. What are the **endpoints / sources**? (Application, Network – routers/switches, Infra, Database)
4. Is the application log in a **proper format**? (JSON, XML, CSV, KV)
5. How **frequently** will the data be used/searched?
6. Are you planning to use any **Machine Learning jobs**?
7. Is **High Availability** required? → decides **replicas**
8. How many **users interact with Kibana** per day?

### Other Things That Add Load
- Watchers
- Dashboards

> ⚠️ **Note:** Replicas **cannot** be created in the **Frozen tier**.

## 6. Sizing Example

**Requirement:** 700 GB/day, retention **45 days** (split as 15 days Hot + 15 days Cold + 15 days Frozen)

### Storage Formula (per tier)

```
Storage = Days × Daily Data × 2 (primary + replica) + 20% overhead
        = 15 × 700 GB × 2  +  (15 × 700 × 2 × 0.2)
        ≈ 25 TB per tier
```

---

### ✅ Solution 1 – Recommended (with Replica / HA)

#### Elasticsearch Nodes

| Tier | Storage | Node Spec | Storage per Node | Nodes Required |
|------|---------|-----------|------------------|----------------|
| **Hot** | ~25 TB | 64 GB RAM, 16 vCPU | ~2.8 TB (taken as **2 TB**) → 25 / 2 | **13** |
| **Cold** | ~25 TB | 64 GB RAM, 16 vCPU | ~12 TB → 25 / 12 | **3** |
| **Frozen** | ~25 TB | 64 GB RAM, 16 vCPU | ~96 TB | **1** *(or 2 nodes with 32 GB RAM, 8 vCPU)* |

#### Total Infrastructure

| Component | Nodes | RAM | vCPU | Disk | Notes |
|-----------|-------|-----|------|------|-------|
| **Elasticsearch** | **18** | as above | as above | as above | Hot + Cold + Frozen |
| **Kibana** | 2 | 16 GB | 8 | 250 GB | 100 GB is enough, but extra space needed if Auditbeat is installed |
| **Logstash** | 2 | 64 GB | 16 / 32 | 700 GB | Needed for **Dead Letter Queue** & **Persistent Queue**; not all data goes through Logstash, but consider 1 day's data |
| **Fleet Server** | 2 | 16 GB | 8 | 200 GB | |

---

### 💰 Solution 2 – Budget Option (No Replica)

| Component | Nodes | RAM | vCPU | Disk |
|-----------|-------|-----|------|------|
| **Elasticsearch** | **7** *(replicas removed)* | — | — | — |
| **Kibana** | 1 | 16 GB | 8 | 250 GB |
| **Logstash** | 2 | 64 GB | 16 / 32 | 700 GB |
| **Fleet Server** | 1 | 16 GB | 8 | 200 GB |

#### Why these changes?
- Reduced **Kibana** and **Fleet** to 1 node each.
- **Logstash kept at 2 nodes** → if Logstash goes down, **no data is ingested** and **no alerts** are generated.
- If **Fleet** goes down, **agents keep working** anyway.

> ⚠️ Always tell the client: **This setup is NOT recommended** by Elastic or by us — it is proposed **only due to budget constraints**.
